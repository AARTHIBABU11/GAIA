GREENLEAF TEXTILES PRIVATE LIMITED
ANNUAL ENVIRONMENTAL & SUSTAINABILITY REPORT 2025-2026
Facility Location: SIPCOT Industrial Complex, Perundurai, Erode, Tamil Nadu

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
GreenLeaf Textiles continues to invest in sustainable manufacturing practices across all processing units. Over the fiscal year 2025-2026, we have actively reduced our environmental footprint through renewable energy adoption, zero liquid discharge effluent treatment, and rigorous raw material recycling.

KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS:
1. Carbon Emissions:
During FY 2025-2026, our total scope 1 and scope 2 emissions were recorded at 420.5 tons CO2 (carbon dioxide equivalent), demonstrating a significant decrease from previous operating cycles. Through energy efficiency audits, boiler economizers, and heat recovery systems, we reduced fossil fuel dependence by 18.4%.

2. Energy & Renewable Adoption:
Our rooftop solar photovoltaic array (1.2 MWp) and wind energy wheeling agreement generated 58.5% renewable energy of our overall power requirements. We are working towards 75% renewable energy transition by 2028.

3. Water Management & Effluent Treatment:
Total freshwater consumption was optimized to 12,450 cubic meters through closed-loop washing machinery. Our biological Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) coupled with Reverse Osmosis (RO) treated and recycled 94.2% of industrial wastewater, achieving near Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD).

4. Solid & Hazardous Waste:
Total solid waste generated across spinning and processing operations was 42.8 tons waste. All non-hazardous cotton lint and yarn wastes were upcycled into packaging materials. Hazardous dye sludge was scientifically co-processed with authorized cement kilns in compliance with Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board (TNPCB) norms.

5. Social Forestry & Biodiversity:
Under our corporate social responsibility charter, the facility planted 1,250 native saplings within the industrial zone perimeter, establishing a green belt buffer.

COMPLIANCE ATTESTATION:
All statutory air emission parameters (PM10, SOx, NOx) and water discharge standards have been consistently monitored through online continuous emission monitoring systems (OCEMS) with uninterrupted telemetry to CPCB and TNPCB servers.
