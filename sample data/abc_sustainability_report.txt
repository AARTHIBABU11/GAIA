ABC MANUFACTURING & CHEMICALS PRIVATE LIMITED
ANNUAL ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT (FY 2025-2026)
Registration No: IND-TN-2024-ABC098
Facility Location: Plot No. 42-45, Phase II, SIPCOT Industrial Park, Ranipet, Tamil Nadu - 632403

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW & COMMITMENT
ABC Manufacturing & Chemicals is dedicated to driving eco-efficient industrial practices and continuous compliance with the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and State Environmental Regulatory Frameworks. During the fiscal year 2025-2026, our site implemented major technological upgrades to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, optimize process water, and transition toward circular resource management.

2. GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS & ENERGY PROFILE
- Carbon Emissions: Our total direct and indirect emissions for FY 2025-2026 were verified at 780.5 tons CO2 equivalent. Through process electrification and high-efficiency boiler economizers, we reduced total carbon output by 14.2% year-over-year.
- Renewable Energy: The facility commissioned a 1.5 MW rooftop captive solar PV plant, currently supplying 52.0% renewable power of our cumulative electrical demand.

3. WATER CONSERVATION & ZERO LIQUID DISCHARGE
- Industrial Water Consumption: Total freshwater withdrawal was audited at 16,800 KL for processing units.
- Wastewater Treatment: The on-site Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP), integrated with multi-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) and a mechanical vapor recompression evaporative system, achieved 96.5% effluent treated and recycled back into manufacturing cycles, preventing ground discharge.

4. SOLID & HAZARDOUS WASTE MANAGEMENT
- Solid Waste Generation: Total non-hazardous process solid waste was recorded at 48.2 tons waste. All recyclable by-products including scrap metals, paper packaging, and plastic liners were routed to certified recyclers.
- Hazardous Waste: Chemical sludge (12.4 MT) was safely packed and transferred to an authorized co-processing cement facility for thermal neutralization in strict conformance with Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules.

5. AFFORESTATION & GREEN BELT INITIATIVE
- Biodiversity: As part of our annual corporate ecological charter, our team planted 850 trees and native botanical saplings within and around the industrial boundary zone, establishing a permanent green perimeter.

6. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE ATTESTATION
Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring (CAAQM) systems and Online Continuous Effluent Monitoring Systems (OCEMS) remain fully operational with real-time data streaming directly to regulatory portals. No statutory exceedances were recorded during this reporting period.
